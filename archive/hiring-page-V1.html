The job

What you would do

If you have worked as a groundskeeper, a ranch hand, or on a landscaping crew, most of this will look familiar. Here are some of the tasks you can expect. They are already written down in a custom app we built for our program, easy to pull up on your phone, with notes on how we want each job done. You will never have to guess.

Two full days covers this list today. You pick which two.

Drag and water the arenas. The outdoor arena, the calf lane, both round pens, and the walker. The covered pen gets picked clean first, then watered, then dragged.
Mow and weed eat. Around the arena, barns, and pens, along the fence lines, the roads, and the entrance.
Spray weeds and brush. Along fences and roads, around the buildings and pens, and the mesquite across the property.
Clean the stalls we mark and scrub the troughs. Bleach water, then refill the areas on your list.
Detail the barns. Rake the alley, pick up manure, sweep the tack room, haul the trash, put tools back where they go.
Keep the equipment running. Fuel, tires, and a look over to make sure it all works. Fill the gas cans in town when they get low.

This is a sample of the work, not the whole list. Tasks run on a repeating schedule and change with the season and what the place needs that week. Every one of them lives in the app with notes on how we want it done, so you will always know what is on your list before you get here.

Why work here

What we promise you

You always know the plan

Every task and project is tracked and managed in our app with notes on how we like it done. There’s no guesswork. Your schedule is set ahead of time.

Pay based on experience

We pay for what you can already do, and we raise it as you take on more. We will talk numbers on the first call.

You get paid biweekly

You clock in and out on your phone, so your hours are logged and your check is never in question. You can see what you worked, what you are owed, and what you were paid before. Payday does not move.

We will teach you

You do not have to know everything on day one. If you want to learn to run a piece of equipment, we will teach you.

There is room to move

Two days can become five. You can take on more responsibility with the arena, the horses, and the cattle. We would much rather move you up than post this job again.

Who we are looking for

You might be the one

  • You show up when you say you will. This matters more to us than anything else on this list.
  • You finish what you start. A job is done when it is done right, not when the clock says so.
  • You take care of tools. You treat our equipment like it is your own.
  • You speak up. If something breaks or does not look right, you tell us instead of letting it slide.
  • You work well around people. We have boarders here. You are friendly, respectful, and helpful.
  • You want more. You are not looking for a job to coast in. You want to learn, take on more, and build something.

You do not need a resume. You do not need extensive horse experience. You do not need to know how to run every machine. Groundskeeper, landscaping, farm, ranch, or maintenance work all carry over here. If you are willing to learn and you work hard, we want to hear from you.
