Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

Resilient Arena LLC operates this website. This policy explains what we collect when you use it, why we collect it, who else sees it, and how long we keep it. It is written to be read, not to be impressive.

Who we are

What we collect

We only collect what you type into a form. We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

Asking about a pen, or joining a waitlist

Your name and phone number, your email address if you choose to give one, which pen you are asking about, and either your preferred lease start date or the question you typed.

The contact form

Your name, email address, phone number, what you are interested in, and whatever you write in the message box.

Reserving the arena

Your name, email address, phone number, the date and time you want, and any notes you add.

Applying for a job

The answers on the application form: your name, contact details, the town you live in, your availability, your work history, your experience with horses and equipment, and a resume if you upload one.

Automatically

Our host keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses and which pages were requested, to keep the site running and secure.

Text messages

If you join the waitlist for a pen, we ask for your phone number so we can text you when that pen becomes available. Giving us your number on that form is how you consent to those messages.

  • What we send. A single message telling you the pen you asked about has opened up, with our phone number so you can claim it.
  • How often. Only when a pen you joined the list for becomes available. Message frequency varies.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
  • Stopping. Reply STOP to any message and you will not receive another. Reply HELP for help, or call us on (325) 627-3726.

No mobile information will be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number and your consent to be texted are used only to send you the waitlist message described above.

Text messages are delivered through Twilio, which acts as our messaging provider and handles the message on our behalf. That is delivery, not marketing, and Twilio does not use your number for its own purposes.

Why we use it

  • To answer your question, arrange a tour, or set up a lease.
  • To hold your arena reservation and contact you about it.
  • To tell you when a pen you are waiting on opens up.
  • To review your job application.
  • To keep the site working, secure, and free of abuse.

We do not sell your information. We do not send marketing emails or texts, and we do not pass your details to anyone who will.

Who else sees it

A small number of companies process information for us so the site can function. They may only use it to provide their service to us.

  • Airtable stores everything you submit. It is where we read your enquiry or application.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps the server logs.
  • Twilio delivers the waitlist text messages.
  • Stripe handles card payments if you pay an arena deposit online. Card numbers go directly to Stripe and never reach us.

Your browser also loads a few things from other companies while the page is open, which means those companies can see your IP address: type from Google Fonts, map tiles from Esri, and the mapping library from unpkg. Opening the driving directions link takes you to Google Maps, which is governed by Google's own policy.

We will also share information where the law requires it, or where we need to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries, waitlist entries, and reservations are kept while we are dealing with them and afterwards as a record of the arrangement. Ask us and we will delete yours.
  • Phone numbers and text message consent are kept for as long as you are on a waitlist, and we keep a record that you opted in, and of any STOP request, so we can honour it.
  • Job applications are kept so we can consider you for future openings.
  • Resumes belonging to applicants we did not hire are deleted automatically twelve months after they apply. This happens on its own, without anyone having to remember.

Your choices

Write to info@resilientarena.com or call (325) 627-3726 and you can:

  • Ask what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct something that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Come off a waitlist, or stop texts without waiting for a message to reply STOP to.

We will not treat you differently for asking.

Keeping it safe

The site is served over an encrypted connection. The keys that let the site write to our systems are held on the server and are never sent to your browser. No system is perfect, but we do not keep information we do not need, and we do not store card numbers at all.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has sent us something, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes to how we use your information will be described here rather than slipped in quietly.
