Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

Resilient Arena LLC operates this website. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us through the forms on this site, such as your name, phone number, email address, and the details of your enquiry, reservation, or job application. Our host also keeps standard server logs so the site can run securely.

How we use it

We use it to respond to you, to arrange boarding or arena time, to consider job applications, and to keep the site working. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Text messages

If you join the waitlist for a pen, we ask for your phone number so we can text you when that pen becomes available. Providing your number on that form is how you consent to those messages.

  • What we send. A message telling you that a pen you joined the waitlist for has become available, and how to reach us.
  • How often. Only when a pen you are waiting on opens up. Message frequency varies.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Stopping. Reply STOP to any message and you will not receive another. Reply HELP for help, or call us on (325) 627-3726.

No mobile information will be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number and your consent to be texted are used only to send you the waitlist message described above.

Messages are delivered by our messaging provider, which handles them on our behalf and does not use your number for its own purposes.

Who else sees it

We use service providers to host the site, store what you submit, deliver text messages, and process payments. They may only use your information to provide those services to us. Your browser also loads fonts and map tiles from other providers while the page is open. We will share information where the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep what you send us for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and to keep a record of the arrangement. Phone numbers and records of text message consent, including any STOP request, are kept for as long as needed to honour your choice. Resumes belonging to applicants we did not hire are deleted automatically twelve months after they apply. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

Contact us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to come off a waitlist. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP or by calling us. We will not treat you differently for asking.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection and we do not store card numbers. No system is perfect, but we do not keep information we do not need.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top.
