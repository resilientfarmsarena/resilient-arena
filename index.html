Boarding & Arena Rental in West Texas

Resilient Arena offers a variety of equine boarding options, including traps, stalls, and pastures, alongside a fully lighted outdoor arena and calf lane, a lighted covered round pen, and a large outdoor round pen. Conveniently located just off I-20, minutes from the Taylor Expo Center and the Abilene Regional Airport.
